About Alafia

A Lifelong Passion for Beauty, Skincare & Bridal Elegance

For Alafia, beauty has never been about masking who you are—it is about illuminating your natural grace so you shine with quiet confidence. From an early love of color, skin health, and traditional aesthetics, she dedicated herself to mastering every facet of professional parlour and bridal artistry.

Whether she is crafting a luminous HD bridal makeover, sculpting clean brows through gentle threading, performing deeply nourishing facials and hair spas, or creating delicate mehndi and nail art, Alafia brings patience, precision, and warmth to every single appointment.

ALAFIA BEAUTY & BRIDAL STUDIO is the realization of her dream: a serene, modern beauty sanctuary where hygiene, high-grade skin-safe products, and personalized one-on-one attention come first.
